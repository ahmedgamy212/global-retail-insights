# 🌍 Global Retail Insights
## Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence project** designed to analyze retail sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and store performance.

The project transforms retail data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards, KPIs, customer segmentation, and profitability analysis.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Understanding retail business performance requires more than tracking total sales. Businesses also need to understand customer value, purchasing behavior, product profitability, and geographical performance.

This project provides a comprehensive analytical view of these areas through a multi-page Power BI dashboard, helping stakeholders explore performance indicators and identify potential business opportunities.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall customer behavior and purchasing patterns.
- Understand customer value and segment customers based on their characteristics.
- Evaluate sales and product profitability.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming products and subcategories.
- Compare profitability across brands and colors.
- Analyze store performance across different countries.
- Explore online and offline business performance.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

The dashboard consists of six main pages:

### 1. Home
A central navigation page providing access to all analytical sections of the dashboard.

### 2. Customer Overview
Focuses on customer behavior and key customer performance indicators.

Key metrics include:
- Customer Churn Rate
- Average Orders per Customer
- Average Profit per Customer
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Total Customers

### 3. Customer Segmentation
Provides deeper insights into customer characteristics and value.

Analytical components:
- RFM Analysis
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Analysis
- Customer Segmentation by Age Group
- Cohort Analysis
- Customer distribution by demographic attributes

### 4. Sales & Product Performance
Evaluates sales performance and product profitability.

Key components:
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Total Profit
- Total Revenue
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Bottom 10 Products by Profit
- Top and Bottom 10 Subcategories by Profit

### 5. Brand & Profitability Analysis
Explores profitability across different product dimensions.

Key components:
- Total Profit by Brand
- Total Profit by Color
- Monthly Revenue and Profit Trends
- Interactive filtering by product attributes

### 6. Store Performance
Analyzes geographical and operational store performance.

Key components:
- Average Profit per Store
- Total Countries
- Total Stores
- Average Profit by Store
- Online vs. Offline Performance

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes several business KPIs, such as:

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Customer Churn Rate | Measures customer loss |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | Evaluates customer retention |
| Average Orders per Customer | Measures purchasing frequency |
| Customer Lifetime Value | Estimates customer value |
| Average Order Value | Measures average transaction value |
| Profit Margin | Evaluates profitability |
| Total Revenue | Tracks overall revenue |
| Total Profit | Measures financial performance |

*Displayed KPI values may change depending on the selected filters and dashboard context.*
